# Vendored AT Protocol Lexicons

The `upstream/app/bsky` and `upstream/com/atproto` schemas are the subset used
by SecretSky's generated client. They are copied from
`bluesky-social/atproto` commit `2f77206ff8010710db5733c454abff9eb5ebb96b`,
which published the corresponding `alpha` package release.

The schemas in `my/` are owned by SecretSky and use the `at.secretsky.*`
namespace controlled through `secretsky.at`.
